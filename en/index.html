FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions and short answers.

Do I need to reserve a table?

We recommend reserving at busy weekend hours; you can leave a request in the form.

Can I order takeaway tea and sweets?

Yes; we prepare flask tea and boxed sweets. Please give a day's notice for group orders.

Are the images and data on the site real?

No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.

Can I get allergen information?

Sweets may contain pistachio, milk and gluten; just tell the staff when ordering.
